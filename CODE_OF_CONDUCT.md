# Code of Conduct

<!-- GrokOff modification (2026-10-08): fork maintainer, reporting route, and independent community expectations. -->

GrokOff follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, commits, and other project spaces.

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, avoid harassment or discrimination, and respect others' privacy. Assume good faith with new contributors, and keep disagreement focused on the work. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct harms the community.

Joe Wilson is the maintainer responsible for enforcement. Use a private contact option listed on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/joewilsonai) to report conduct concerns. If none is available, request a private contact method without posting incident details or personal information publicly. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent practical, and retaliation against reporters is unacceptable.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Upstream maintainers and their contact addresses are not GrokOff's reporting channel.
